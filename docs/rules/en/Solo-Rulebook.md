## 1. What solo play uses

Everything in the box, plus the 12 **Ghost cards**. The Ghost is an unseen companion: it holds no cards, and at the end of each turn a Ghost card is turned over to tend the river for you.

## 2. Setting up

Set up as in section 3 of the *Rulebook*, with only these differences:

<div class="rulebox" markdown="1">

| Item | Solo play |
|---|---|
| Graves in Reach 3 | 2 |
| Commissions to fulfil | 1 |
| Hand (and hand limit) | 6 cards |
| Where the Scar markers start | Cell 10 of the knot track (the pale teal cells) |
| Ghost deck | Shuffle the 12 Ghost cards and put them face down to one side |

</div>

- **Commission**: you draw just 1 Commission card, and must fulfil it.
- **Oar**: it stays in front of you.
- "Round" in the glossary: in solo play, a round is one of your turns.

## 3. Your turn

Play as in section 4 of the *Rulebook*; only Swapping is different:

<div class="rulebox" markdown="1">

### 3-1. Swapping in solo play

Put 1 card from your hand on the bottom of the Memory deck, turn over the top card of the Memory deck and play it face up at once, following section 4-1 of the *Rulebook*, Resonance and Echo inscription included. You may Swap at most once per turn.

</div>

## 4. The end of the turn: the Ghost

Carry out steps 1–7 of section 5 of the *Rulebook*; after step 7 (drawing) and before passing the Oar, the Ghost acts:

<div class="rulebox" markdown="1">

### 4-1. The Ghost acts

1. Turn over the top card of the Ghost deck and carry out its action (section 7).
2. Tie 1 knot on that Ghost card's thread. If the thread becomes full, Backlash at once.
3. Move the Echo marker to that Ghost card's thread.
4. Clean up the river once more (step 6 of section 5 of the *Rulebook*).
5. Put the Ghost card on the Ghost discard pile. When the Ghost deck runs out, shuffle the discard pile into a new Ghost deck.

</div>

## 5. The Loom thresholds

Solo thresholds are higher: the Scar marker starts on cell 10 of the knot track and moves one cell down for each Scar. The pale teal cells 10 / 9 / 8 are the solo threshold cells.

| Scars on this thread | Threshold | When it becomes full |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 10 | Level I Backlash |
| 1 | 9 | Level II Backlash |
| 2 | 8 | Level III Backlash |

## 6. Mantles

In solo play you may change Mantles:

- You may summon a new Mantle even while you hold one; turn your old Mantle card face down, and it can never be summoned again.
- Each Mantle may be summoned only once per game, and you may summon at most 3 times per game.
- Summoning still costs Light and a discarded card of the Mantle's thread.

## 7. Ghost cards

| Ghost card | Number | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Obsession | 3 | Place a Root on the furthest empty bank ahead of the soul |
| Obsession | 2 | Place a Root on the nearest empty bank ahead of the soul |
| Acceptance | 2 | The nearest Root Blooms |
| Acceptance | 2 | The furthest Root Blooms |
| Oblivion | 2 | Banish the nearest Shade, or drain the nearest Black Water |
| Oblivion | 1 | No action |

When the Ghost places a Root, it follows the rules for placing a Root: the bank must connect to the Root network. "Nearest" and "furthest" are as defined in the *Rulebook*'s glossary.
