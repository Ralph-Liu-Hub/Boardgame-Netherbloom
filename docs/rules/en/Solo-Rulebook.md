## 1. How solo play works

You are the only Ferryman: you guide the soul across Lethe and write their Book of Remembrance. Winning and losing are as in section 1 of the *Rulebook*: you win when the soul reaches the Far Shore with your 1 Commission fulfilled, and you lose if the game ends in Faded, Shattered, Deluge or Lost.

**The Ghost** is an unseen companion: it holds no cards, and at the end of each of your turns a Ghost card is turned over to tend the river for you; it also ties a knot on the Loom and moves the Echo marker.

There are no other players, so the Veiled Memories do not apply; a "round" in the glossary is one of your turns.

## 2. What you need

Everything in the box (Appendix A of the *Rulebook*), plus the 12 **Ghost cards** (section 8).

## 3. Setting up

### Choosing a Soul and a difficulty

First choose a Soul (section 13 of the *Rulebook*), then one of these difficulties.

| **Difficulty** | ◆◇◇ Gentle | ◆◆◇ Standard | ◆◆◆ Hard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fog when the Mist advances | +0 | +1 | +1 |
| When the soul is Engulfed by the Mist | Mist scar only | Mist scar only | Mist scar, and the soul takes 1 Fracture |
| Starting knots | 0 | 0 | 1 on each thread |
| Faded limit | 14 cards forgotten | 12 cards forgotten | 12 cards forgotten |
| Shattered limit | 5 Fractures | 4 Fractures | 3 Fractures |
| Black Water | 7 | 6 | 6 |

**Solo set-up**:

| Item | Solo play |
|---|---|
| Graves in Reach 3 | 2 |
| Commissions to fulfil | 1 |
| Hand (and hand limit) | 6 cards |

**A first game for new players**: we suggest playing **the Child of the Last Flame** at Gentle.

### Set-up steps

1. **River boards**: join the 3 river boards in order: Reach 1 · the Asphodel Road (the Near Shore, space 0, and spaces 1–4), Reach 2 · the Fields of Mourning (spaces 5–8), Reach 3 · the Banks of Lethe (spaces 9–13, ending at the Far Shore, space 14).
2. **Bank tiles**: set up each of the three Reaches separately. Space 1 has no tiles.
    1. Take out the number of Graves each Reach needs and put them beside its tile pool: none for Reach 1, 1 for Reach 2, and 2 for Reach 3.
    2. Shuffle each Reach's tile pool (Appendix A of the *Rulebook*) face down, and draw as many as its banks need: 6 for Reach 1, 7 for Reach 2, and 8 for Reach 3 (10 less the 2 Graves).
    3. Shuffle each Reach's Graves and drawn tiles together face down, and lay them at random, fog side up, on that Reach's banks.
    4. Return the tiles not drawn to the box without looking at them.
    5. Put the spare tiles (2 Flower Field, 2 Fertile, 2 Memory Spring) face up to one side. Fate Mist and Mist cards use them to replace tiles.
3. **The first Blooms and the soul**: put 1 token, Bloom side up, on the left bank and on the right bank of space 1. Put the soul marker on the Near Shore (space 0). The fog begins after space 0; no bank has been swallowed by the fog yet.
4. **Soul card**: put the chosen Soul's card face up next to the board. Its special rule is in effect for the whole game.
5. **Memory deck**: shuffle the 32 basic memory cards together with the chosen Soul's 8 own memory cards, and put them face down in the deck frame of the Ledger. When the soul is "Open Crossing", use the 8 Echo cards instead.
6. **Mist decks**: the Mist cards form three decks (Appendix C of the *Rulebook*): Pale Mist for chapter 1, Thick Mist for chapter 2, and Deep Mist for chapter 3.
    1. Add 1 "Rising Water" and 1 "Shade Drawn Near" to each deck, then add 2 harsh Mist cards at random. Return the other harsh Mist cards to the box.
    2. Shuffle the three decks separately and put them face down to one side.
    3. Put the Pale Mist deck in the Mist well, turn its top card over, and put it face up in front of the well: this is the **Mist ahead**. The back of the next card of the Pale Mist deck can also be seen: it shows a thread and a Fog number.
7. **Loom of the Fates**: set the Loom out beside the river board. Put 1 knot marker at 0 on the knot track of each of the three threads; at Hard, put it at 1 instead. Then put 1 Scar marker on cell 10 of the same knot track (the pale teal "Scar 0" cell: the solo threshold, see section 6 of this book). Put the Echo marker in the Loom's Silent box.
8. **Mantles**: lay the 5 Mantle cards face up in the Mantle row along the top of the Supply.
9. **Book of Remembrance**: open the chosen Soul's book at chapter 1, then split this Soul's 12 Fate Mist cards into three groups by chapter, 4 for each chapter: 2 with river conditions and 2 with Book conditions. Draw 1 at random from chapter 1's river conditions and 1 from its Book conditions, and put them condition side up beside the Book; put the rest face down to one side. Put the ending cards face down to one side without looking at them. The soul "Open Crossing" has no ending cards.
10. **Ledger**: put Black Water markers in the Black Water supply as the difficulty says (7 for Gentle, 6 for Standard and Hard). Put the Light marker on the Light track at the chosen Soul's starting Light (section 13 of the *Rulebook*; 0 if none is given). Put the Fracture marker at 0 on the Fracture track. Put the Root/Bloom tokens, Shades, fog markers, and the Hold Fast, Shield and Resignation markers and the Root network trait marker in the supply.
11. **Commission card**: shuffle the chosen Soul's 8 Commission cards, draw 1 and put it face down in front of you, where you may look at it. Put the rest face down to one side for redraws. You must fulfil this one.
12. **Ferryman**: draw a hand of 6 cards. Take 1 Push Through token, blue side up. The Oar stays in front of you all game.
13. **Ghost deck**: shuffle the 12 Ghost cards and put them face down to one side (section 8 of this book).

When the set-up is done, begin your first turn.

## 4. Your turn

You play every turn yourself. On each turn, in order:

1. **Before your first action**, you may do any of these, none of them an action, in any order:
    - **Summon a Mantle**: pay Light and **discard 1 card from your hand of the Mantle's thread** (section 9 of the *Rulebook*).
    - **Redraw your Commission**, once per game: remove your Commission card from the game face down, and draw 1 new one.
    - If you hold the **Reaper**, use its ability.
2. **First action (may Resonate)**: play 1 card face up (section 4-1), play 1 card face down (section 4-2) or Swap 1 card (section 4-3); or Recall (section 4-4).
3. **Second action (never Resonates)**: play 1 card face up, play 1 card face down or Swap 1 card; or Recall.
4. **Light and Commissions**, both optional:
    - **Light memories**: for each 1 Light you pay, light 1 memory in the Book, as many per turn as you like (section 10 of the *Rulebook*).
    - **Reveal a fulfilled Commission** (section 12 of the *Rulebook*).
5. **The end of the turn** (section 5).

Across your two actions you may Swap at most once and Recall at most once per turn. You may use the free action **Push Through** at any time (section 4-5).

### Ways to play a card

|  | **Face up** | **Face down** | **Swap** | **Recall** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Effect | The card's effect | Place 1 Root; if there is nowhere to place it, gain 1 Light instead | Play the card you receive face up | Pay 3 Light to inscribe 1 Obsession or Acceptance card from the forgotten pile back into the Book |
| Knot | +1 on the card's thread | +1 on the card's thread | As face up | None |
| Echo | Moves to the card's thread (on the first or the second action alike) | Does not move | As face up | Does not move |
| Resonance | Only the first action may Resonate, and only if the Echo marker was already on the card's thread | Never | As face up | Never |
| May be inscribed | If the Echo was already on the card's thread; a Key Memory always may | No | As face up | Always inscribed |
| Where to put the card | Inscribed in the Book; otherwise Obsession and Acceptance to the discard pile, Oblivion to the forgotten pile | Face up on the discard pile | As face up | In the Book, unlit |

### 4-1. Playing a card face up

In order:

1. Resolve the card's effect.
2. On the Loom, tie 1 knot on the knot track of the card's thread. If the thread becomes full, **Backlash at once first**, then go on to the next step (section 7 of the *Rulebook*).
3. Check which thread the Echo marker was on **before** you played this card. If it is the same as this card's thread:
    - and this is your first action, resolve the card's **Resonance**. A Resonance marked "instead" replaces the normal effect.
    - and this is your second action, you cannot resolve the card's Resonance.
    - Whichever action this is, you may **inscribe** the card in an empty memory slot with a matching slot type on the current route of the Book, and read its memory aloud. This is called an **Echo inscription**.
4. Move the Echo marker on the Loom to this card's thread.
5. If the card was not inscribed, put it on the discard pile if it is an Obsession or Acceptance card, and on the forgotten pile if it is an Oblivion card.

A card with a **Key Memory**, whenever it is played face up, may have its memory inscribed, whichever thread the Echo is on.

</div>

> **Example**: the Echo is on Acceptance. As your first action you play an Obsession card: the Echo is not on Obsession, so the card does not Resonate and cannot be Echo-inscribed; the Echo moves to Obsession. As your second action you play another Obsession card: the Echo is already on Obsession, so this one may be inscribed. It does not Resonate, because Resonance belongs only to the first action.

<div class="rulebox" markdown="1">

### 4-2. Playing a card face down

Ignore the effect printed on the card and place 1 Root instead (section 6 of the *Rulebook*). If there is no empty bank where a Root can be placed, gain 1 Light instead. Whether you placed a Root or gained Light, tie 1 knot on the card's thread. The Echo marker does not move; put the card face up on the discard pile.

</div>

<div class="rulebox" markdown="1">

### 4-3. Swap (solo play)

Put 1 card from your hand on the bottom of the Memory deck, turn over the top card of the Memory deck and play it face up at once, following the steps above, Resonance and Echo inscription included.

- You may Swap at most once per turn.

</div>

<div class="rulebox" markdown="1">

### 4-4. Recall

Recalling takes an action, at most once per turn. Pay 3 Light, choose 1 Obsession or Acceptance card from the forgotten pile, inscribe it in an empty memory slot with a matching slot type on the current route, and read its memory aloud. This memory is unlit.

- It may only go into an empty memory slot on the current route. Slots the route has not reached yet, closed branches and earlier chapters are not allowed; if there is no matching empty slot, you cannot Recall that card.
- Oblivion cards cannot be Recalled.
- A forgotten Key Memory can be brought back.
- Recalling does not move the Echo marker and ties no knot.
- The forgotten pile is 1 card smaller: move the forgotten marker back 1 on the forgotten track.

</div>

<div class="rulebox" markdown="1">

### 4-5. Push Through (free action)

- **When to use**: at any time, when **both** banks of the next space have a Shade and the next space has no Black Water.
- **Effect**: return both Shades to the supply and move the soul onto the next space. When the soul leaves their old space, choose to gather or leave the light as usual. Flip your Push Through token face down. Then the soul takes 1 Fracture.
- It takes no action and does not move the Echo marker. You may do it once per game.

</div>

## 5. The end of the turn

After each of your turns, carry out these steps in order:

1. **Bloombringer**: if you hold the "Bloombringer", you may make 1 Root Bloom for free.
2. **Soul Step**: follow the steps below (section 5-1).
3. **Shades push the Loom**: for each Shade on the soul's space, tie 1 knot on the Loom: on Obsession for one on the left bank, on Acceptance for one on the right bank, and on Oblivion for a Shade in the fog.
4. **Mist**:
    1. Carry out the instructions on the Mist ahead.
    2. **The Mist advances**: only if the soul did not move forward this turn. The number of banks it advances is the Fog of this Mist card plus the difficulty's bonus (section 6 of the *Rulebook*).
    3. **Check for engulfing**: if both banks of the soul's space are fog side up, the soul is Engulfed by the Mist: the Mist card just carried out becomes a Mist scar (section 10 of the *Rulebook*). At Hard difficulty, the soul also takes 1 Fracture.
    4. Put the Mist card on the Mist discard pile unless it became a Mist scar. Turn over the top card of the Mist deck: it is the new Mist ahead. When the deck is empty, take this chapter's Mist cards (Pale, Thick or Deep Mist) from the Mist discard pile and shuffle them into a new Mist deck; the harsh Mist cards in the discard pile, and the 1 Rising Water and 1 Shade Drawn Near added at set-up (any card of that name), go back to the box and are not used again this chapter. Mist cards that became Mist scars stay in the Book.
5. **The fork and turning the page** (section 11 of the *Rulebook*):
    - If the route stops before the turning point and a Fate Mist card is already on the ◆: the story forks at once.
    - If the route has reached an exit: turn the page at once.
6. **Cleanup**: check the river space by space:
    - **Black Water**: on every space with Black Water and a Root or Bloom on **both** banks, remove the Black Water from the game and put it back in the box.
    - **Shades**: return to the supply every Shade not in the fog that has a Bloom on the same side both on the space directly ahead **and** on the space directly behind.
7. **Draw** back up to your hand size.
8. **The Ghost acts**: turn over the top Ghost card and carry it out (section 5-4).

### 5-1. The Soul Step

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A{Resignation marker<br>beside the soul?} -- Yes --> A1[They do not move; forget the top card<br>Remove the Resignation marker] --> E
    A -- No --> B{Is the next space Lit<br>and free of Black Water?<br>Gate of the Far Shore: section 11}
    B -- Yes --> W{Does the "Spirit Warden"'s holder<br>make them Stay?}
    W -- Yes --> W1[The soul stays on their space<br>No card is forgotten<br>Turn the "Spirit Warden" sideways] --> E
    W -- No --> B1[The soul moves forward 1 space<br>The space they left: gather or leave the light] --> C
    B -- No --> D[The soul Lingers<br>Carry out the first of I–V that applies] --> E
    C{How many cards forgotten?} -- 6–8: Light as Ash --> C1[If the next space is Lit and free of Black Water<br>move forward 1 more space] --> E
    C -- 9 or more: Adrift --> C2[Always move forward 1 more space<br>If the next space is unlit or has Black Water<br>move anyway and take 1 Fracture] --> E
    C -- 5 or fewer --> E
    E[Shade damage: a Shade on either bank of the space<br>where the soul ends, and they take 1 Fracture]
```

1. **Resignation**: if the Resignation marker is beside the soul, they do not move; forget the top card of the deck and remove the Resignation marker. Go to step 5.
2. **Can the soul move forward?** If the next space is Lit and has no Black Water, the soul moves forward 1 space; if you hold the "Spirit Warden", you may make them Stay. Until the third chapter of the Book is written, the Far Shore counts as unlit (section 11 of the *Rulebook*).
3. **Leaving a space**: every time the soul leaves a space, choose to gather or leave the light, including extra moves, card effects and Push Through. If you gather, the Blooms on that space wither to Roots; if you leave the light, the Blooms stay. Either way, no token is taken off that space: its Roots, its withered Roots and its Shades stay on their banks, and go on joining the Root network, holding back the fog or pushing the Loom.
4. **Extra move**: if the soul moved forward this turn, look at the number of cards in the forgotten pile:
    - 6–8, Light as Ash: if the next space is Lit and has no Black Water, move forward 1 more space.
    - 9 or more, Adrift: always move forward 1 more space. Even if the next space is unlit or has Black Water, they move anyway and take 1 Fracture. If you hold the "Spirit Warden" and it is not turned sideways, you may stop this extra move: the soul does not move and takes no Fracture.
5. **Shade damage**: once the soul has finished all of this turn's moves (extra moves included), check the space where they ended: if either bank has a Shade, the soul takes 1 Fracture. Spaces passed on the way do not count. Check this whether they moved forward, Stayed, were Resigned or Lingered.

**Lingering**: when the soul cannot move forward, check from I in order and carry out only the first that applies:

- I. The Hold Fast marker is beside the soul: forget nothing.
- II. You hold the "Spirit Warden" and it is not turned sideways: turn the "Spirit Warden" sideways; forget nothing.
- III. The soul's space is Lit: forget nothing.
- IV. The soul is the Bride Who Stayed Below and has not used their special rule this game: they are pulled back one space and forget nothing (section 13 of the *Rulebook*).
- V. None of the above: forget the top card of the deck.

</div>

### 5-2. At the start of the next turn

- If the Hold Fast marker is out, remove it.
- The "Spirit Warden" and the "Mistbreaker" you hold turn upright at the start of your own turn; only then can their abilities be used again.
- A Shield marker that has not cancelled a Fracture is removed at the end of your next turn.

</div>

<div class="rulebox" markdown="1">

### 5-3. Effects that happen at once

- A thread that becomes full Backlashes at once; then go on with what you were doing.
- A Fate Mist card is revealed as soon as its condition is met (section 11 of the *Rulebook*).
- The game ends at once when: the soul reaches the Far Shore (go on to the Shore-Keeper's question); the forgotten pile reaches the Faded limit; Fractures reach the Shattered limit; you must place Black Water but the supply is empty.

</div>

<div class="rulebox" markdown="1">

### 5-4. The Ghost acts

1. Turn over the top card of the Ghost deck and carry out its action (section 8 of this book).
2. Tie 1 knot on that Ghost card's thread. If the thread becomes full, Backlash at once.
3. Move the Echo marker to that Ghost card's thread.
4. Clean up the river once more (step 6 of the end of the turn).
5. Put the Ghost card on the Ghost discard pile. When the Ghost deck runs out, shuffle the discard pile into a new Ghost deck.

</div>

## 6. The Loom thresholds

Solo thresholds are higher: the Scar marker starts on cell 10 of the knot track and moves one cell down for each Scar. The pale teal cells 10 / 9 / 8 are the solo threshold cells. For the Backlashes and their timing, see section 7 of the *Rulebook*.

| Scars on this thread | Threshold | When it becomes full |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 10 | Level I Backlash |
| 1 | 9 | Level II Backlash |
| 2 | 8 | Level III Backlash |

## 7. Mantles

In solo play you may change Mantles (the other rules are in section 9 of the *Rulebook*):

- You may summon a new Mantle even while you hold one; turn your old Mantle card face down, and it can never be summoned again.
- Each Mantle may be summoned only once per game, and you may summon at most 3 times per game.
- Summoning still costs Light and a discarded card of the Mantle's thread.

## 8. Ghost cards

| Ghost card | Number | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Obsession | 3 | Place a Root on the furthest empty bank ahead of the soul |
| Obsession | 2 | Place a Root on the nearest empty bank ahead of the soul |
| Acceptance | 2 | The nearest Root Blooms |
| Acceptance | 2 | The furthest Root Blooms |
| Oblivion | 2 | Banish the nearest Shade, or drain the nearest Black Water |
| Oblivion | 1 | No action |

When the Ghost places a Root, it follows the rules for placing a Root: the bank must connect to the Root network. "Nearest" and "furthest" are as defined in the *Rulebook*'s glossary.
