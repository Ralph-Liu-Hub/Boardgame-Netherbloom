# Netherbloom — Player Aid

> **You lose at once if:** **Faded**: the forgotten pile reaches your Lantern's ✕ (8–11) · **Shattered**: the 3rd Fracture well is filled · **Deluge**: Black Water must be placed and its well is empty. **You win** when the soul reaches the Far Shore.

### On your turn

1.  **Summon**: optional · pay Light, discard a matching card. Solo: you may trade your Mantle for a new one (the old is laid down for good), 3 summons a game
2.  **Answer**: your first action: a card played face up or Burned on the Echo's thread Resonates
3.  **Steer**: your second action. Either may be a Push Through (once a game for each Ferryman); Burn at most once a turn
4.  **End of turn**: 1 Bloom-Bringer · 2 Soul Step: next space Lit and no Black Water, move; otherwise Linger · 3 Mist: flip the top card of this Reach's deck · 4 Cleanup · 5 Draw up · 6 Solo: Ghost · 7 Pass the Oar
5.  **Cleanup**: remove Black Water where both banks have a disc; remove a Shade with a Bloom on the same bank before and after it

### Play a card

- **Face up**: effect + knot + new Echo
- **Face down**: place 1 Root; no knot, Echo stays
- **Burn**: once a turn, with a player holding the same thread: both reveal a card, you pick one; effect ×2 + knot + new Echo; forget both, they draw 1. Solo: one card, then forget it and the top card
- **Recall**: your Answer only; 3 Light: a forgotten card of your choice returns to the discard pile
- **Push Through**: Shades on both banks of the next space (no Black Water): remove both and move the soul there; 1 Fracture (+1 per face-down Push Through token), forget 1; once a game for each player

### Threads (4–5 knots)

- **Obsession**: Full: Shade on the soul's space
- **Acceptance**: Full: the soul does not move in the next Soul Step
- **Oblivion**: Full: Black Water on the next space. Played cards go to the forgotten pile

### Roots must touch

The soul's space or the next one, the opposite bank, or the same bank next door.

### Forgetting

- **6**: Light as Ash: +1 move onto a Lit space
- **9**: Adrift: +1 move; onto a dark space it takes 1 Fracture
- **8–11**: Faded: the game is lost

### Tokens

- Oar: whose turn
- Push Through: 1 each
- Hold Fast
- Shield
- Resignation

Discard pile and forgotten pile are both face up; the forgotten pile lies on the Ledger.
