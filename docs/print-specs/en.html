Netherbloom — Print Specifications V3.2

Every printed component: finished size, size with bleed, printing, stock and file. Measures in millimetres.

General rules

  • Every PDF page is the finished (trim) size plus 3 mm bleed on each side, plus a 10 mm slug with crop marks, registration targets and a colour bar.
  • Keep text and important art at least 3 mm inside the trim (the safe area).
  • Colour: CMYK, Coated FOGRA39 (ISO 12647-2:2004, ISO Coated v2 condition), total ink 330%.
  • Card fronts and backs share one page size; every card back lines up head to head with its front.
  • Die-cut tokens and bank tiles carry 2 mm of art past every cut.
