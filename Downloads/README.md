Click a file, then **Download** (or "View raw"). Each zip opens on its own.

What changed in V3.1:
- River board panels are 266 × 182 mm (was A3), each with a Mist deck well and 42 mm square wells.
- The 20 bank cards are now 40 × 40 mm bank tiles (`tiles/`), fog on the back.
- The soul disc is gone from the punchboard (77 tokens); the soul is a fold-up standee, five on one A4 sheet (`standees/`).
- All card and board text is at least 10.5 pt; the Player Aid is now 3 double-sided A5 cards.
- 正體中文 wording revised (面朝上／面朝下、洗混、河岸板塊、亡魂立牌).

The factory files are split in two per language because GitHub refuses files over 100 MB.
`SHA256SUMS.txt` lets you check a download is complete (`shasum -a 256 <file>` on a Mac).
