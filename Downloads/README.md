Every PDF page is the finished size plus **3 mm bleed**, converted to **CMYK** (Coated FOGRA39) with a 10 mm slug holding
crop marks, bleed marks, registration targets, a colour bar and a page label; TrimBox and BleedBox are set. Every flat
component fits the V3.2 box (285 × 285 mm or smaller). The files are built by `print/v5` (see its README) from the same
data as the web table and checked by `print/v5/check.py`. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums.

concept sketches; replace them in `print/v5/art/` and rebuild.
