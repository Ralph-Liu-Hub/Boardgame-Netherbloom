What changed in V3.2:
- Rulebook V3.2 in 正體中文 and English (American English, standard board-game wording).
- Keepsake cards (36: 9 per named Soul, 3 per Reach) and ending cards (19: A–D per named Soul plus three hidden endings) are in the box, with a Keepsake back and a Story back. A Memory Site draws a keepsake for that Reach's Book page; at the end each page's keepsakes are read after it, and on a win the matching ending card is read last. They never change who wins.
- Loom of the Fates and Ledger boards are 285 × 210 mm (were 297) so they lie flat in the 296 mm box base; every token and card well keeps its size.
- The factory zips are now split as cards, boards and tokens (part 1) and Books, box and rulebook (part 2), to stay under GitHub's 100 MB limit.

What changed in V3.1:
- River board panels are 266 × 244 mm (they lie flat in the 296 mm box base). The river runs unbroken across all three; each Reach's Mist deck well, sized for the deck lying sideways, sits in a strip along the left bank, and every bank has a 42 mm square well.
- The 20 bank cards are now 40 × 40 mm bank tiles (`tiles/`), fog on the back.
- Factory PDFs are press-ready: **CMYK** (Coated FOGRA39), **3 mm bleed** on every page (die-cut tokens and tiles carry 2 mm of art past every cut), and a 10 mm slug with crop marks, bleed marks, registration targets, a colour bar and a page label; each page has its TrimBox and BleedBox.
- Every two-sided piece is checked: each tile back, turned over left to right, sits exactly on its front and its cut; each token JPG has a back of the same name and size, centred; card fronts and backs share one page geometry. `MANUAL.pdf` section 3 says how to print each file.
- Page numbers on every page of the rulebook, Player Aid and Books of Remembrance (covers excepted).
- All card and board text is at least 10.5 pt; the Player Aid is now 3 double-sided A5 cards.
- 正體中文 wording revised (面朝上／面朝下、洗混、河岸板塊).

Tokens: the factory gets them as single JPGs, not a punchboard sheet. `tokens/jpg/25mm/` holds the 25 mm tokens (Oar, Push Through, Hold Fast, Shield, Resignation, Soul; 9 tokens, 18 JPGs on a 31 × 31 mm canvas, 732 px) and `tokens/jpg/18mm/` the 18 mm tokens (Root-Bloom, Shade, Black Water, Fracture, Light, Knot, Echo; 69 tokens, 138 JPGs on a 24 × 24 mm canvas, 567 px). 600 dpi, Coated FOGRA39 profile embedded, quality 100 without chroma subsampling, each token centred at true size; fronts and backs share a name, e.g. `Soul Front 1.jpg` / `Soul Back 1.jpg`. A README in each folder lists the copies of each token. `MANUAL.pdf` section 6 says how to place them.

The factory files are split in two per language because GitHub refuses files over 100 MB.
`SHA256SUMS.txt` lets you check a download is complete (`shasum -a 256 <file>` on a Mac).
